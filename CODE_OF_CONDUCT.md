# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want this project to be a respectful, constructive place for contributors and users of every background, identity, experience level, and perspective.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, specific, and generous in technical and language feedback.
- Discuss ideas and evidence, not a person's worth or identity.
- Respect privacy, cultural context, and the dignity of people represented in examples.
- Welcome good-faith questions and acknowledge uncertainty when it exists.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate misinformation, publishing private information, or disruptive conduct are not acceptable.

## Reporting concerns

For conduct concerns, contact [thisaimentor10@gmail.com](mailto:thisaimentor10@gmail.com). Reports will be handled as privately and fairly as practical.
